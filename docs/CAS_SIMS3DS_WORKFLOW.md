# Create-A-Sim in The Sims 3 (Nintendo 3DS): the workflow, from the reference video

Source: the user's gameplay video "The Sims 3D - Create A Sim Nintendo 3DS Gameplay" (59 frames, one every 3 seconds, 640x360).
Written so any port (PS1, Dreamcast, DS) can copy the same flow. Frame numbers are f_001 .. f_059.
This file is about the 3DS game. How BORE 2 uses it is at the end (section 8).

## 1. The two screens
- TOP screen: a 3D room (window with pink curtains, wooden table with a flower pot, a green plant, an orange sofa). The Sim stands on a round white platform in the middle. It is always live: each change shows at once.
- BOTTOM (touch) screen: the menu. One panel with a title bar, a tab rail on the left, the options in the middle, an X (close) button top right.
- Everything on the bottom screen is tapped with the stylus. Big, round, glossy buttons. Blue and white, a little purple. Plain short words.
- While a new model loads a ring of small white dots spins over the button you pressed (f_014, f_026, f_030, f_034).

## 2. The steps, in order (the left tab rail, top to bottom)
The rail is a column of round icon buttons. The lit one is the open page. You can tap any tab at any time, in any order. The normal path is down the rail.

| # | Tab (title bar) | Rail icon | What is on it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | (welcome) | - | One pop-up: "Welcome to Create A Townie! Here, you can create a Sim to add to your town. You can also share these Sims with others in Sims Exchange." One tick button (f_002). |
| 1 | Basics | two linked gender signs | Name, Gender, Fitness, Randomize, Skin Color (f_003) |
| 2 | Looks | head side-view | Capture Face (camera button) and Edit Features (face picture). Edit Features opens the face editor (f_015 to f_023) |
| 3 | Hair | brush | Hair styles, facial hair, hats, colour dots (f_024 to f_033) |
| 4 | Clothing | person in a jacket | Outfit sets and single pieces (f_034 to f_048) |
| 5 | Personality | plumbob (green diamond) | 3 traits, Lifetime Wish (f_049 to f_057) |
| 6 | Summary | tick on a clipboard (bottom of the rail) | Everything on one page, then finish (f_058) |
| end | Save | - | "Would you like to save your game?" Yes / No (f_059) |

A round button above the rail (top left, two small people) is the home button of the editor.

## 3. Page by page

### 3.1 Basics (f_003 to f_014)
- Title bar "Basics", X top right.
- Name: first name box and last name box side by side, a dice icon above them (random name). Tap a box: the keyboard page (3.7).
- Gender: two big square buttons, female sign and male sign. Tapping one swaps the whole model on the top screen at once (f_009 to f_010: a woman becomes a man in a red jacket) with the loading ring.
- Fitness: two body buttons, a slim figure and a round figure. Changes the build live (f_013 to f_014).
- Randomize: one button "Create a random Sim." Rolls everything.
- Skin Color: a row of round colour dots (6 shown, the chosen one has a white ring). The page scrolls down to it.
- Top screen: the whole body, front, standing still.

### 3.2 Looks (f_015 to f_023)
- Two big buttons: Capture Face (camera, uses a photo) and Edit Features (a face picture).
- Edit Features: the top-screen camera swoops in to the face (f_015 blurry, f_016 close).
- Bottom: the sub-page title is in the middle ("Edit Ears and Head", "Edit Eyebrows", "Edit Eyes", "Edit Nose", "Edit Mouth"). The same layout on every one:
  - LEFT: a vertical strip of 5 shape pictures (a scroll arrow on top when there are more). The chosen one is boxed. These are the presets of that part.
  - MIDDLE: a big square with a cross in it and a small dot. Drag the dot to move or size that part (up/down = place, left/right = width). The face on top follows.
  - RIGHT (Eyes only): 4 round colour dots for the eye colour (yellow, blue, grey, green: f_019 blue eyes, f_020 green).
- The sub-pages go in this order: Ears and Head, Eyebrows, Eyes, Nose, Mouth (the video steps through them one after another).
- Top screen stays zoomed on the face for the whole page (head and shoulders).

### 3.3 Hair (f_024 to f_033)
- Title "Hair". Under it TWO ROWS of small icons:
  - Row 1 (4 icons): hair, hair extras, facial hair (face icon), hats. The lit one decides what the grid shows. Facial hair (f_026): 3 faces, clean, goatee, full beard. Hats (f_030): 4 hats.
  - Row 2 (4 to 5 icons + a padlock + a tick box): filters of that list (styles by kind), the padlock shows or hides locked things.
- Right of those: four small colour dots (the hair colour, brown, red, yellow, black).
- BELOW: a grid of thumbnails, 4 across, scrolls up and down. Each thumbnail is the style on a head. The chosen one has a blue frame.
- Top screen: the camera is on the head and shoulders. Each pick shows at once (f_025 black quiff, f_029 bald, f_032).
- The Sim keeps its clothes while you pick hair (the shirt colour changes only because the clothing changes).

### 3.4 Clothing (f_034 to f_048)
- Title "Clothing". Same two icon rows as Hair.
  - Row 1 (4 icons): outfit CATEGORY: everyday, formal, sleepwear, swimwear / underwear. The top screen shows the full outfit for that category (f_034 red suit, f_040 brown leather jacket, f_042 plaid pyjamas, f_043 to f_044 underwear and swim shorts).
  - Row 2 (5 icons + padlock + tick box): the PART you change: full outfit, top, bottom, shoes (and more). Under it the grid (4 across) of pieces: suits (f_035), shirts (f_036, f_045), trousers (f_037), shoes (f_038 to f_041).
- The top camera follows the part. Full outfit: whole body. Top: closer on the chest (f_035, f_047). Bottom: legs (f_037, f_038). Shoes: down at the feet with the platform in view (f_039, f_041). Then it pulls back out.
- The model changes at once with the loading ring. The Sim also shows a small idle move (f_048 a lean).

### 3.5 Personality (f_049 to f_057)
- Title "Personality". Top row: a dice "Random" button, then THREE square "?" slots (the three traits), then a round button (Change / Accept).
- Tap a "?" slot: the trait LIST (f_050 to f_054). A scrolling list of wide rows. Each row: icon, trait name, a tick box. Neat, Slob, Genius, Natural Cook, Virtuoso, Charismatic, Unflirty, Friendly, Mean Spirited, Good Sense of Humor, No Sense of Humor, Athletic, Couch Potato... Up and down arrows and a scroll bar on the right.
  - Tick a trait: its icon fills the next "?" slot at the top (f_053 a mask, f_054 two traits). A trait that fights with a ticked one is greyed out (No Sense of Humor, f_053).
  - Up to 3 may be ticked. "Accept" (big tick, top right) closes the list.
- After the traits: Lifetime Wish. A row of 5 choices (small icons) and the chosen one big below it with its name (f_055 to f_057).
  - Tap a wish: the TOP screen shows a card: icon, name, points (2,000) and a short description (f_055 "Swimming in Cash: have $50,000", f_056 "Power Broker: reach the top of the Business career", f_057 "Mad Scientist"). The bottom shows its name under the big icon.
  - A "Random" dice rolls 5 new wishes. A tick confirms the wish.
- Top screen: a plain view of the Sim, with the wish card over it.

### 3.6 Summary (f_058)
- Title "Summary", the Sim's full name just under it.
- A row of 4 round icons (Basics, Looks, Hair, Clothing: tap one to go back and change it).
- Two panels side by side: "Lifetime Wish" (the big icon) and "Personality" (the three trait icons).
- A tick box "Add to Sims Exchange" (share online).
- A big round TICK, bottom right: finish.
- Top screen: the finished Sim, full body, standing.
- Then a pop-up: "Would you like to save your game?" Yes / No (f_059).

### 3.7 Name keyboard (f_005 to f_013)
- Opens on its own page: title "Enter First Name" (or "Enter Last Name"). A text bar with the name, a backspace arrow beside it.
- A keyboard: row of digits 1 to 0, then Q to P, A to L, Z to M and a dot. Below: Shift (becomes Symbols), a wide Space, and a tick button at the bottom right to accept. X top right closes.
- Typed text appears in the bar. On the top screen the Sim keeps showing live (and the name changes only on the tick).
- The first name and last name each have their own keyboard page; after the first tick you return to Basics.

## 4. Navigation rules (what to copy)
1. One tab rail, always visible, down the left. The open tab is lit. Any tab at any time.
2. One title bar with the page name. X top right = leave (it asks first).
3. Every choice shows on the model at once. No "apply" button.
4. The camera on the top screen is part of the page: Basics full body; Looks and Hair head and shoulders; Clothing follows the piece (chest, legs, feet); Personality plain view with a card; Summary full body.
5. Choices are big pictures in a grid or a strip (4 across), or round colour dots. Text is short and the same size everywhere.
6. Randomize exists in three places: whole Sim (Basics), name (the dice by Name), personality (dice) and wishes (dice).
7. Names get a typed keyboard page; the keys are big.
8. Finish: the Summary page and a tick. Then a save question.
9. No back and next buttons: you move by the rail. (BORE 2 adds BACK and NEXT because a pad has no stylus: see below.)

## 5. Screen layout numbers (bottom screen, 320x240 equivalent)
- Rail: about 24 px wide on the left, 6 or 7 round buttons of about 20 px.
- Title bar: 16 px high, the name centred, X button on the right.
- Content: about 270 x 200. Gaps 4 to 6 px. Section titles are small, with a thin line under them.
- Thumbnails about 38 x 38, 4 across. Swatches about 18 px round.

## 6. Timing seen in the video
- Welcome pop-up, then Basics: name, gender, fitness. About 40 s.
- Looks: about 25 s. Hair: about 25 s. Clothing: about 40 s. Personality: about 30 s. Summary and save: 10 s.

## 7. What the Sims 3 has that a pad cannot do
Stylus drag on the face grid, the photo capture, the online share box. Replace with: left/right on a pad, the sliders in the game, a plain ON/OFF.

## 8. How BORE 2 follows it (PS1 and Dreamcast)
One screen, a pad. The 3D studio is the preview (left side), the panel is on the right.
- Steps (the rail, shown as a numbered strip): 1 BASICS, 2 LOOKS, 3 HAIR, 4 CLOTHES, 5 STYLE (wear and parts), 6 PERSONALITY, 7 SUMMARY.
- L1/R1 (or BACK / NEXT) change step. D-pad up/down pick a row, left/right change its choice (the carousel), TRIANGLE randomise, SQUARE name keyboard, X NEXT, O BACK, START = jump to the summary.
- Each step has the camera of the Sims 3: full body, face close, head and shoulders, chest, legs, feet.
- Name keyboard, randomise, the summary with a tick and the "keep this Bore?" question.
